How to use this app

This app is a plan for your money. You put money into categories (a box of money for one thing), then you spend from those boxes. Everything saves to your Google Drive, so your phone and your computer show the same budget.

Words you will see
CategoryA box of money for one thing, like Rent or Food. Also called an envelope.
CheckMoney that comes in, like your paycheck.
SplitSharing one amount of money between more than one category.
TransactionMoney going out (an expense) or money coming in (income).
PeriodThe stretch of time you are budgeting for, like this month.
Set it up once — do these in order

Step 1. Make your categories.

Open Settings, type a name in Categories, then click Add. It saves as soon as you add it. Use the things you spend money on: Rent, Food, Gas, Phone, Fun, Savings. You can add more later.

Step 2. Add your checks.

Open Paychecks and click + New check. Type a name (like "Friday pay"), the take-home pay (the money that really lands in your account), then split that money into categories. Example for a $1,500 check:

CategoryAmount
Rent900.00
Food300.00
Gas120.00
Fun80.00
Savings100.00
Total1500.00

The parts must add up to the check amount. Save it and the app keeps it, so next time you just pick the check. Do this for each check you get.

Step 3. Enter today's balance.

Open Transactions and set Type = Income. Description: Starting balance. Amount: the money in your checking account right now. Split that money into categories the same way you split your check. This tells the app how much is in each box today. After this, the Categories tab shows your real numbers.

Order matters: categories first, then checks, then today's balance. After that you just keep track of money going in and out.
Everyday use

You spent money

On Transactions, fill in the form at the top: Type = Expense, the date, what it was, the amount, and the category. Click Add.

You got paid

Transactions → Type = Income → choose your check in Which check?. The name, amount and split fill in for you. Change anything that is different this time, then Add.

You are not sure what category something is

Pick Needs Review as the category. It waits for you on the Needs Review tab, where you can give it a real category or split it later.

You want to see how you are doing

Categories shows what is left in each box. Green means you still have money there. Red means you went over. Report shows where the money went this period.

When a period ends (like at the end of the month)

Click New period at the top. It saves a backup, moves what is left in each category into the new period, and starts fresh. Put in the new start date, check the numbers, then click Close & start new. A category in the red stays in the red (you still owe it) unless you type a different number. The old transaction details are cleared, so download a copy first if you like to keep one (Settings → Data → Download .db).

Phones, computers, and your data
  • Your changes save by themselves. When you are signed in, they also go to Google Drive a few seconds later.
  • Sign in with the same Google account on your phone and on your computer and you get the same budget. A change made on one shows up on the other in a few seconds while both are open.
  • If the top of the screen says the sign-in expired, open Settings and click Sign in with Google once.
  • The badge at the top tells you what is going on. Synced 3:42:05 PM means Google Drive has your latest copy. Offline or Sync error means the app keeps trying in the background; your changes are still safe in this browser.
  • Do not change the budget on two devices at the same moment. If you do, the last save wins and the other change is lost.
  • To fix a mistake, use the buttons on the transaction row: change the category, split it, edit it, or delete it.
  • Settings → Data → Download .db saves a copy of your budget on your device. Start fresh makes a new empty budget for every device; it uploads a backup of the old one first.
